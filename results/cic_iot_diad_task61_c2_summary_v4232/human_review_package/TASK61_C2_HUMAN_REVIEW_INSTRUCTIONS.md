# Task 61 blinded human review

Two independent reviewers are required. Review items in display order and do not open the private blinding key or private analyst answer key until both score sheets are complete.

For every item, score factual accuracy, clarity, usefulness, trustworthiness, and uncertainty quality from 1 to 5. Record the answers you infer for the five analyst questions: plain attack rate, paired clean rate, recovery fraction, whether malicious intent may be claimed, and whether reserved test arrays were materialized. A coordinator compares those answers with the private key and records the number correct from 0 to 5.

Do not discuss scores with the other reviewer until both files are frozen. Human review completion and statistical comparison occur in Task 61 C3.
